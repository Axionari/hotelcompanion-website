A guest may explain a complicated arrival plan more easily by speaking. Another may prefer to type quietly, compare room photographs or review a total before confirming. Good hospitality makes room for each preference.

### Keep the journey connected
A guest should be able to speak, type or browse without losing the details already provided. Dates, party size and selected services need to remain visible and editable.
Manual operation needs a clear route too: browse, choose, review and confirm. A conversation should make that route easier to use.

### Confirm what matters
Before a reservation or purchase, show the dates, guests, prices, terms and next action. Distinguish a request sent to the team from a booking that has been confirmed.
Keep a route to a person when the request is sensitive, unclear or outside the agreed scope. Explain what information reaches the team.

### Test the channels you plan to launch
Web access can begin with a link or QR, without an app download. Telephone, messaging and optional in-room devices require their own connection and operating plans.
Try the full journey on the actual phone and desktop layouts. Success means the guest can reach a useful outcome through their preferred method.
