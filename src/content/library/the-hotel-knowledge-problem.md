Your team knows which room has the terrace, how to arrange a late arrival and who handles an urgent request. Making that knowledge easier to use can reduce repeated explanations and improve consistency.

### Give information an owner
Start with rooms, services, prices, policies and destination guidance. Name the person who can approve each area and review changes before publishing.
Record when an answer depends on live availability or a person’s confirmation. A reliable companion needs a clear route when information is missing or out of date.

### Build around real requests
Use the questions your team receives every day. Test combined requests, exceptions and changes of plan. Confirm that the answer leads to an appropriate next step.
The aim is to make useful knowledge available during service, with the right context for the employee or guest using it.

### Make upkeep manageable
Agree a review routine and a route for staff to flag errors. Track repeated questions and corrected answers to identify where information needs attention.
Begin with one department or guest journey. Measure the usefulness of answers, handoff quality and the effort required to keep knowledge current before expanding.
