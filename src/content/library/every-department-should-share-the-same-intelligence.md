The guest sees one hotel. A request may involve reception, housekeeping, engineering or food and beverage. Clear ownership helps those teams deliver a connected experience.

### Pass the useful details
Share the request, relevant room or booking information and the next action the guest expects. Give staff enough context to help without asking the guest to start again.
Keep access appropriate to role and property. Shared service context does not require giving everyone unrestricted access to guest information.

### Name the owner
Decide who receives the request, who can approve an exception and how another shift takes over. A visible status helps managers distinguish received work from acknowledged, in-progress and resolved work.
When a connected system is not available, use an agreed team route and explain which step still requires confirmation.

### Review the handoffs
Look for unclear ownership, repeated explanations and requests that remain open. Rehearse common and difficult cases before launch.
Start with one cross-department journey. Review resolution quality and staff effort as well as speed, then use what the team learns to improve the next handoff.
