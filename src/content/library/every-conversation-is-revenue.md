“Could we have a quieter room?” “Can we arrange dinner before we arrive?” These questions can open a commercial opportunity. The value depends on helping the guest choose something useful and delivering it well.

### Recommend what fits
Start with expressed needs. Match them to verified room features, current services and approved prices. Give the guest a clear choice and respect a decline.
A celebration can make a dinner relevant. It does not establish a budget or permission to add purchases. Resolve complaints and service problems before making another offer.

### Count completed business
Keep enquiries, recommendations, acceptances and completed purchases separate. Reconcile changes, cancellations and refunds with the source records.
A purchase handled through the companion is not automatically an additional purchase. Compare eligible outcomes against an agreed baseline or suitable comparison group.

### Review the contribution
Consider delivery costs, discounts, channel costs and benefits offered. Then account for platform and implementation fees when reviewing the hotel’s net benefit.
Staff time freed is useful capacity. Treat it as cash savings when actual costs fall.
Start with one eligible service or upgrade journey. Agree the comparison and costs before launch so both teams can assess the result.
