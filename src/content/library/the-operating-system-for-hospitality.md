A hotel already uses systems for reservations, orders, payments and guest records. A companion becomes useful when it helps people act across the parts included in the agreed guest journey.

### Start with approved knowledge
The companion needs current room details, services, prices and policies. Each area needs an owner and a review process.
Guest context adds the details relevant to the request. Identity, permission and retention determine what can carry into another visit or channel.

### Connect an authorized action
Define the supported actions for each PMS, POS, payment or service connection. Validate access, provider requirements and the source of confirmation before enabling transactions.
Human approval and clear team routes remain part of the operating design. Show the guest what is requested, pending and confirmed.

### Make outcomes reviewable
Companion Control brings the request, owner and status into the team’s view. Commercial reporting needs completed source records and an agreed method for measuring added contribution.
Begin with one property and one useful journey. The platform’s value becomes clearer as the hotel proves which connections and workflows improve service and business results.
