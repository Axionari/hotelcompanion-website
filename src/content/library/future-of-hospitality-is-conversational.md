A guest asking about a room, a transfer or dinner is trying to make a decision. A helpful answer creates value when the next step is easy to complete.

### Begin with the guest’s goal
Understand the dates, party and preferences that matter. Present relevant options from the hotel’s approved information, then let the guest review the choice.
Voice can make a complex request easier to explain. Text and visual controls let guests browse, compare and continue at their own pace. The same journey should work without an app download.

### Carry the context into service
If a person must confirm availability or arrange the request, pass the useful details to the right team. Give the work an owner and keep its status visible.
An answered enquiry, an accepted recommendation and a completed booking are different outcomes. Record each stage so managers can see where guests need help.

### Prove one useful improvement
Start with one journey: direct-booking enquiries, an airport transfer or a recurring service request. Agree a baseline and review completed outcomes, guest feedback and the effort required from the team.
The question to bring to a demonstration: where does a guest who is ready to act currently get stuck?
