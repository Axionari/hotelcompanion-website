A good answer helps a guest understand. Good service also helps the hotel act. Evaluating a companion means following the request beyond the conversation.

### Follow a real request
Take a familiar example: the air conditioning needs attention. The companion should gather the relevant details, route the request and tell the guest what happens next.
The hotel team needs the room, issue, priority and conversation context. Someone must own the work, update its status and record the resolution.

### Make control visible
Decide which answers use approved knowledge, which actions need approval and when a person takes over. A confirmation should reflect a recorded outcome from the team or an agreed connection.
Test unclear requests, unavailable services and changes of plan. The quality of the handoff matters as much as the first response.

### Measure the work that improves
For operations, useful measures include unresolved requests, handoff quality and time to resolution. For commercial journeys, track completed purchases and contribution after costs.
Ask the vendor to show the guest’s view and the team’s view of the same request. That demonstration reveals whether the next step is clear for everyone.
