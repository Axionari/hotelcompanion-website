A guest in another time zone may enquire while your reservations desk is closed. During a busy arrival period, another may leave a question unanswered. Timely assistance can keep those conversations moving.

### Extend the first response
Use approved hotel knowledge to help with questions and guide guests to the next available action. Capture useful details for the team when a person must confirm the request.
A service enquiry after hours is different from fulfillment after hours. State opening times, availability and expected follow-up clearly.

### Support the shift
Routine questions can be handled through the agreed channels while staff focus on arrivals and requests that need attention. Keep the handoff visible so work does not disappear between systems or shifts.
Define escalation and urgent-contact routes before launch. Requests requiring professional judgment or emergency support need the appropriate human response.

### Measure recovered opportunity
Record enquiries handled during closed or busy periods, then follow them to completed outcomes where evidence permits. Review conversion, service quality and team workload against a baseline.
Begin with the hours and journey where the gap is clearest. Make responsiveness useful, with an honest account of what happens next.
